## Lesson Structure Requirements

Each lesson MUST include all 13 sections:

1. **Header**: One-liner, Time, Deliverable, Prerequisite
2. **Learning goal**: Single measurable capability
3. **Success criteria**: Observable binary checks
4. **Output you will produce**: Exact deliverable specification
5. **Who**: Primary persona, Secondary personas, Stakeholders
6. **What**: What it is, What it is not, 2-minute theory, Key terms
7. **Where**: Applies in, Does not apply in, Touchpoints
8. **When**: Use it when, Frequency, Late signals
9. **Why it matters**: Practical benefits, Risks of ignoring, Expectations
10. **How**: Step-by-step method, Do and don't, Common mistakes
11. **Guided exercise**: Step-by-step with expected result
12. **Independent exercise**: Task without guidance
13. **Self-check**: Observable verification checklist
14. **Bibliography**: Real sources with URLs
15. **Read more**: Additional resources with explanations

## Planned Lessons

### Day 1: AI Decision Framework - When to Use AI vs Traditional Solutions

**Learning Goal**: You will be able to: **Evaluate a business problem and determine whether AI or traditional programming is the appropriate solution, using a systematic decision framework.**

**Deliverable**: AI Suitability Assessment Checklist

**Key Topics to Cover**:
- Pattern recognition vs rule-based problems
- Data availability and quality requirements
- Accuracy expectations (80-99% vs 100%)
- Cost-benefit analysis for AI projects
- Hybrid approaches

**Research Sources to Reference**:
- Harvard Business Review: AI adoption frameworks
- Google's Rules of Machine Learning (Martin Zinkevich)
- Andrew Ng's AI Transformation Playbook
- Industry case studies (successful and failed AI projects)

**Exercises**:
- **Guided**: Walk through evaluating email spam detection
- **Independent**: Evaluate 3 different business scenarios
- **Self-check**: Apply framework to learner's own use case

**Quiz Questions**: 7-10 application/critical-thinking questions about decision scenarios

---

### Day 2: Working with AI Tools - Practical Implementation

**Learning Goal**: You will be able to: **Select appropriate AI tools for common tasks, evaluate their output quality, and integrate them effectively into existing workflows.**

**Deliverable**: AI Tool Evaluation Matrix

**Key Topics to Cover**:
- Categories of AI tools (text, image, code, data)
- Evaluation criteria (accuracy, speed, cost, privacy)
- Prompt engineering basics
- Output validation and quality checks
- Integration patterns

**Research Sources to Reference**:
- OpenAI documentation and best practices
- Anthropic's Constitutional AI principles
- Practical AI case studies from businesses
- Tool comparison studies and benchmarks

**Exercises**:
- **Guided**: Test an AI writing tool with structured evaluation
- **Independent**: Evaluate 2 AI tools for a specific task
- **Self-check**: Create evaluation criteria for learner's use case

**Quiz Questions**: 7-10 application questions about tool selection and validation

---

### Day 3: AI Best Practices - Ethics, Safety, and Continuous Improvement

**Learning Goal**: You will be able to: **Implement responsible AI practices including bias detection, output verification, user transparency, and iterative improvement strategies.**

**Deliverable**: AI Implementation Checklist with Safety Guidelines

**Key Topics to Cover**:
- Common AI failure modes and limitations
- Bias detection and mitigation
- Human-in-the-loop approaches
- Transparency and explainability
- Monitoring and continuous improvement
- Ethical considerations

**Research Sources to Reference**:
- EU AI Act guidelines
- IEEE Ethics in AI standards
- Research on AI bias (MIT, Stanford)
- Industry best practices (Microsoft, Google AI Principles)
- Real-world AI incidents and lessons learned

**Exercises**:
- **Guided**: Audit an AI output for bias and errors
- **Independent**: Create safety checklist for a specific AI application
- **Self-check**: Review learner's AI usage against best practices

**Quiz Questions**: 7-10 critical-thinking questions about ethics, safety, and real-world scenarios
